# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in this project are expected to:

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, and decisions rather than people.
- Accept good-faith corrections and make space for quieter participants.
- Protect private information, credentials, and security-report details.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or
using project infrastructure to target another person.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an
already-established private channel. Do not open a public issue containing
personal information or security-sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities
must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.

Reports are reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may
remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action
necessary to protect project participants and infrastructure.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
